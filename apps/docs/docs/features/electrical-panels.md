# Electrical Panels

Domitara can map out your home's electrical panel(s) so you always know what a breaker
actually controls before you flip it.

![Electrical panel viewer](/img/web/electrical-panel.png)

## Panels

A home can have multiple panels — a main panel plus one or more subpanels. Each panel records:

- Name (e.g. "Main Panel", "Garage Subpanel")
- Total amps and total slots
- A location note (e.g. "Basement utility room")
- An optional parent panel, for subpanels fed from another panel

## Breakers

Click any slot in the panel diagram to add or edit a breaker: its amperage, what it controls
(a room, an appliance, an outlet run), and notes. Breakers can also be tagged with a **floor
plan area** — a named, color-coded zone (see below) — so the panel view doubles as a rough map
of what circuit covers what part of the house.

## Floor plan areas

Define named areas with a color (e.g. "Kitchen" in orange, "Garage" in blue) under a panel's
floor plan area manager, then assign breakers to them. This is useful for larger panels where
"which breaker is the kitchen on" is otherwise a guessing game.

## Printing a directory

**Print directory** generates a printable breaker directory for the selected panel — the kind
of sheet you'd tape inside the panel door — listing every slot, its breaker, and what it
controls.

## Deleting a panel

Deleting a panel removes all of its breakers. This cannot be undone, so double-check you have
the directory printed or otherwise recorded first if you need a record of the old layout.
