# Homes & Members

Domitara is built around **homes** — you can track more than one property (a primary
residence, a rental, a vacation cabin) under the same account, each with its own items,
locations, labels, photos, documents, electrical panels, and members.

Switch between homes with the home picker in the top-left of the header. Everything else in
the app — items, locations, maintenance, panels — is scoped to whichever home is currently
active. Use **Add home** from that same menu to create another one.

![Home details](/img/web/home-details.png)

A home's own page (click its name in the sidebar) has six tabs:

## Details

Basic info (name, property type, address, year built, square footage, acreage), financial
info (purchase price/date, estimated value, mortgage lender and notes), and HOA info (name,
contact, monthly dues). All optional beyond the home's name.

## Photos

A simple gallery for exterior shots, renovation before/afters, or anything else worth keeping
a visual record of for the property itself (as opposed to individual item photos).

## Documents

Upload and categorize home-level paperwork: deed, insurance policy, inspection report,
survey/plat, HOA documents, warranties, permits, tax records, floor plans, or "other". Each
document can be typed after upload so they're easy to filter later.

## Members

Invite other users to a home by email — they'll get **member** access (an **owner** is
whoever's home it is, and can't be removed). Removing a member revokes their access to that
home's data; it doesn't delete their account.

## Electrical Panels

See [Electrical Panels](./electrical-panels).

## Floor Plans

Upload floor plan images per level (basement through floor 4) and draw named, color-coded
zones directly on them. These zones are the same **floor plan areas** used to tag electrical
breakers, so a zone you draw here can double as "what part of the house is this breaker
actually in."

## Deleting a home

Only an owner can delete a home, from the **Danger zone** at the bottom of the Details tab.
This permanently removes every item, location, label, photo, document, and electrical panel
for that home, for every member — there's no undo.
