# Maintenance schedule ideas

A starting checklist of common recurring home-maintenance tasks, grouped by how often they
typically need doing. Not every item applies to every home (a lot depends on climate, whether
you're on well water vs. municipal, gas vs. electric, etc.) — treat this as a menu to pick
from when setting up [schedules](./maintenance#schedules), not a mandatory list.

For each one you decide to track, create a schedule with a title, a frequency, and (if it
applies to a specific appliance) a linked item — e.g. link "Replace HVAC filter" to your
furnace item so the reminder and its history live together.

## Monthly

- **Check/replace HVAC filter** — monthly for standard fiberglass filters, or per the
  manufacturer's rating for pleated/high-MERV filters (often 60–90 days)
- **Test smoke & carbon monoxide detectors** — press the test button; full battery
  replacement is usually annual (see below)
- **Inspect fire extinguishers** — check the pressure gauge and expiration date
- **Run water through unused drains** (guest bathroom, basement floor drain) to keep P-traps
  from drying out and letting sewer gas in
- **Clean garbage disposal** and check for odors

## Quarterly (every 3 months)

- **Clean refrigerator condenser coils** — vacuum or brush off the coils (usually behind a
  bottom kick-plate or on the back) so the compressor isn't overworking
- **Clean range hood / microwave grease filter**
- **Deep-clean washing machine** — run a hot/cleaning cycle, wipe the door gasket and
  detergent drawer (especially front-loaders)
- **Test garage door auto-reverse safety feature**
- **Inspect and clean dryer vent duct** (not just the lint trap — the actual duct to the
  exterior) if you do a lot of laundry; otherwise this can move to semiannual

## Semiannual (every 6 months)

- **Flush the water heater tank** — drains sediment buildup that reduces efficiency and
  lifespan
- **Replace furnace/AC filter** if not doing it monthly
- **Clean dryer vent duct** (see above)
- **Rotate mattress**
- **Deep-clean behind/under large appliances** (fridge, stove)
- **Check water softener salt level** and refill
- **Test sump pump** — pour water into the pit and confirm it kicks on and drains
- **Re-caulk around tubs, sinks, and windows** where seals look cracked or discolored
- **Change whole-home water filter cartridge** (if installed)

## Annual

- **Clean gutters and downspouts** — more often (spring + fall) if you have a lot of nearby
  trees
- **Service HVAC system** — professional tune-up for furnace (fall) and A/C (spring)
- **Replace smoke & CO detector batteries** (unless 10-year sealed units)
- **Chimney sweep and inspection** if you have a wood-burning fireplace or stove
- **Inspect roof and flashing** for damaged or missing shingles, especially after storms
- **Service/inspect water heater** (anode rod check on tank units — every 2–3 years is also
  common)
- **Pressure-wash siding, deck, and walkways**
- **Reseal/stain the deck** or check existing sealant for wear
- **Aerate and overseed the lawn**
- **Sharpen and service lawn mower blades**; winterize gas equipment before storage
- **Sprinkler system startup (spring) and blowout (fall)** in freeze-prone climates
- **Pool opening (spring) and closing (fall)** if applicable
- **Inspect exterior paint/caulk** for peeling or gaps
- **Test/inspect septic tank** and schedule pumping (every 3–5 years depending on household
  size and tank capacity)
- **Test well water quality** if on a private well
- **Garage door and opener lubrication** (rollers, hinges, chain/screw drive)
- **Check attic and crawlspace insulation and ventilation**
- **Vacuum bathroom exhaust fan grilles** and confirm fans are still venting outside properly

## As-needed / condition-based

- **HVAC filter** — replace sooner than scheduled if visibly dirty
- **Water heater anode rod** — replace when significantly corroded (usually every 3–5 years)
- **Caulking and weatherstripping** — replace at first sign of drafts or cracking
- **Roof repairs** — after any major storm
- **Appliance filters** (fridge water filter, range hood charcoal filter) — per manufacturer
  indicator or every 6 months as a default

---

Once you've decided which of these apply to your home, add each as a schedule from the
[Maintenance](./maintenance) screen — the reminders and completed-log history will build up
automatically as you check things off.
