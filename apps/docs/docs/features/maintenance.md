# Maintenance

The **Maintenance** screen tracks anything you need to do to your home or your stuff on a
repeating basis — replacing filters, servicing equipment, seasonal chores — plus a log of
what's already been done.

![Maintenance screen](/img/web/maintenance.png)

It has three parts:

## Reminders

Auto-generated nudges — a schedule that's overdue or due soon, or a general suggestion (like
"add photos to your items"). Each reminder can be **dismissed** or **snoozed** for 1 day,
3 days, or a week.

## Schedules

A schedule is a recurring task: a title, a frequency (e.g. "every 3 months", "every 1 year"),
and optionally a linked item (so "Replace HVAC Filter" points at your actual furnace item).
Schedules show a status pill — **Overdue**, **Due soon** (within 7 days), or **Upcoming** —
computed from their next due date.

- **Add schedule** to create one from scratch
- The menu on each row lets you **Edit** or **Delete** it
- **Mark done** (the checkmark) opens the log-maintenance form pre-filled with that schedule's
  title and item, and — once logged — pushes the schedule's due date forward by its frequency

Not sure what to put on a schedule? See [Maintenance schedule ideas](./maintenance-schedules)
for a list of common recurring home tasks by frequency.

## Recent logs

A history of completed maintenance: date, title, item, and cost. Logging maintenance directly
(via **Log maintenance**) doesn't require a schedule — you can record one-off work too (a
repair, an inspection, anything worth having a paper trail for). Individual log entries can be
deleted from their row menu.

Maintenance logged against a specific item also shows up in that item's own **Maintenance**
tab on its detail page.
