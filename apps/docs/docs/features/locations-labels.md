# Locations & Labels

Domitara gives you two independent ways to organize items: **locations** (where something
physically is) and **labels** (tags for cutting across locations — "kitchen appliances",
"under warranty", "to sell").

## Locations

Locations form a tree — rooms can contain sub-locations, which can contain further
sub-locations. Each location is one of two types:

- **Room** — a regular location that can have child locations nested inside it (e.g.
  *Basement* → *Utility Closet*)
- **Container** — a drawer, cabinet, or shelf unit with a defined grid (rows × columns).
  Containers show their contents as a visual grid you click into cell-by-cell, and — because
  they represent a physical grid rather than a room — **can't have locations nested inside
  them**.

![Locations screen](/img/web/locations.png)

Click any location in the tree to see its item count, breadcrumb, and the items stored there.
From there you can rename or delete the location (deleting one moves its items to
"unassigned" and promotes any child locations to the top level rather than deleting them too),
or drill into **View all items** for the full paginated list.

## Labels

Labels are flat (no nesting) and color-coded. Use them for anything that doesn't map cleanly
to a physical place: `insured`, `holiday decor`, `loaned out`, `to donate`. An item can carry
any number of labels alongside its single location.

![Labels screen](/img/web/labels.png)

Click a label to see every item tagged with it, rename the label, change its color, or delete
it (deleting a label just removes the tag from items — it doesn't touch the items
themselves).

## Choosing locations vs. labels

A good rule of thumb: if the answer to "where is it?" changes, it's a **location**. If the
answer to "what kind of thing is this, or what's its status?" changes, it's a **label**. Most
homes end up with a handful of top-level rooms, a few containers for small stuff (junk
drawers, toolboxes, medicine cabinets), and a small set of reusable labels.
